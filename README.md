# Hyundai Kona & Mitsubishi ASX asking prices

Interactive chart and sortable table for public Yad2 listings, model year 2020 onward: Hyundai Kona (petrol, hybrid and electric) and Mitsubishi ASX (all petrol in this snapshot). The ASX was added at the same 2020+ cutoff as the Kona.

Snapshots: 9 October 2026. 389 unique listings: 282 Kona (132 petrol, 121 hybrid, 29 electric) and 107 ASX. Both models and the petrol + hybrid fuels are selected by default; use the independent model, fuel and km-range checkboxes to change both chart and table.

Filled markers are Kona, open markers are ASX. Marker shape identifies fuel: circle for petrol, diamond for hybrid, square for electric. Point color runs from green (low mileage) to red (high mileage); the on-page color key labels all six bands. Unknown mileage is gray.

Mileage comes from Yad2's own result-page km filter, not an exact odometer reading. Bands: 0-30K, 30-60K, 60-90K, 90-120K, 120-150K, 150K+. These labels abbreviate inclusive query boundaries 0-30000, 30001-60000, 60001-90000, 90001-120000, 120001-150000, and 150001 upward. Representative band midpoints choose colors only. The open-ended top band is capped at the red end of the visual scale and does not assert actual mileage. Kona band counts: 54, 73, 60, 64, 21, 10. ASX band counts: 20, 27, 26, 20, 8, 6.

Kona refreshed against the 8 October snapshot: 31 added (29 electric, 2 petrol/hybrid), 5 no longer present. Presence changes are not proof of a sale. Listings were deduplicated by public ad token; promoted duplicates were not counted twice. No individual listing pages were opened and no login was used. One Kona listing had no link available in the result page, so its link is marked unavailable.

Hover for listing details, click a chart point to open the ad, or use the sortable table. Listings without a price remain in the table. Raw result-page price values below ILS 10,000 (8 Kona, 9 ASX) are retained and flagged in the table but excluded from the chart. Their meaning (monthly offer, placeholder or another reason) was not verified, so they must not be treated as full car asking prices. Chart-eligible prices: 259 Kona and 98 ASX; 331 in the default both-models petrol/hybrid view. Year positions are slightly spread to separate overlaps. Links can expire, and individual ad links were not independently opened. Prices are asking prices, not sale prices. Fuel/trim variants are mixed; this is not a valuation.

The page loads Plotly from a public CDN and needs internet access. Personal analysis, not affiliated with Yad2. The page and repository are public; no personal owner information, seller contact details or images are included.
